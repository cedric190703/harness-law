1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Frontier Geosciences & Environmental, LLC ("Frontier") was retained by Rocky
Environmental Services, Inc. ("RES" or the "Company") to conduct a Phase II
Environmental Site Assessment (the "Phase II ESA") of the Grand Junction
Industrial Yard located at 4820 Highway 6 & 50, Grand Junction, Colorado (the
"Site"). The Phase II ESA was performed to evaluate potential soil and
groundwater impacts identified as recognized environmental conditions in the
previously completed Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (dated May 2019) and
to assess the nature and extent of impacted media in areas of historical
industrial use at the Site.

The Site has been used for industrial purposes for several decades, including
equipment storage, maintenance and staging, fueling, and the handling of
regulated and non-regulated materials associated with field operations.

This document is an executive summary of the Phase II ESA report. It is intended
to be read in conjunction with the full report and its appendices, which are
included in the data room under the "Environmental" tab.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SUBSURFACE INVESTIGATION SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Phase II ESA scope of work included:

    (a)  advancement of soil borings and collection of soil samples at
         multiple depths across the yard and near historical fueling, storage
         and maintenance areas;

    (b)  installation of temporary groundwater monitoring points and collection
         of groundwater samples;

    (c)  laboratory analysis for volatile organic compounds (VOCs), semi-
         volatile organic compounds (SVOCs), total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH),
         polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs) and select metals, as appropriate to
         the historical uses identified; and

    (d)  a screening-level comparison of analytical results to applicable
         Colorado soil and groundwater standards.

3. FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  Soil. Analytical results indicate the presence of constituents in soil
     above applicable screening levels in several areas of the Site. Constituents
     detected in the impacted areas are consistent with historical industrial and
     maintenance activities conducted at the Site.

3.2  Nature of impacts. The historical laboratory data and site conditions
     evaluated by Frontier are consistent with contamination resulting from
     gradual releases associated with long-term historical operations, rather
     than a discrete, sudden or accidental release event. The observed
     distribution of constituents in soil is consistent with gradual
     contamination that has occurred over an extended period.

3.3  Groundwater. Groundwater was encountered beneath the Site at depths
     evaluated during the assessment. Detected constituents and general
     groundwater conditions suggest that groundwater beneath portions of the
     Site may be impacted. Frontier recommended additional groundwater
     characterization to further delineate conditions.

4. ESTIMATED REMEDIATION COSTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Based on the conditions identified and the remediation assumptions known as of
June 2019, Frontier estimated the range of costs to implement a program to
address the identified soil conditions at the Site to be approximately
$1,800,000 to $2,600,000.

That estimate was based on the information, conditions and assumptions known at
the time the Phase II ESA was prepared, including:

    (a)  the nature and extent of contamination identified as of the assessment
         date;

    (b)  assumptions regarding the potential for contaminant migration and the
         area of impacted media requiring management;

    (c)  assumed groundwater conditions and the potential scope of any
         groundwater management activities;

    (d)  assumed excavation, handling, transportation and off-site disposal
         costs for impacted media, including landfill tipping fees; and

    (e)  then-applicable federal, state and local regulatory requirements and
         agency expectations.

The estimate did not reflect, and did not attempt to predict, potential changes
in conditions, contaminant migration, disposal costs, regulatory requirements
or remediation approaches that could arise after the date of the assessment.
No updated or current remediation cost estimate has been prepared for the Site.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Frontier recommended that RES (i) perform additional delineation of soil impacts,
(ii) conduct further groundwater characterization, (iii) evaluate remediation
alternatives with respect to the identified conditions, and (iv) maintain
appropriate regulatory coordination. The recommendations were provided for
planning purposes.
