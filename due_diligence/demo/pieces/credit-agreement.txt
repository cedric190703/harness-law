ARTICLE I - DEFINITIONS AND ACCOUNTING TERMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1.01  Defined Terms.

"Applicable Margin" means, with respect to SOFR Loans, 2.35% per annum, and with
respect to Base Rate Loans, 1.35% per annum.

"Borrower" means Rocky Environmental Services, Inc., a Utah corporation.

"Change of Control" means the occurrence of any of the following: (a) any
"person" or "group" (within the meaning of Sections 13(d) and 14(d) of the
Securities Exchange Act of 1934) becomes the beneficial owner, directly or
indirectly, of 35% or more of the equity interests of the Borrower entitled to
vote for the election of directors; (b) during any period of twelve consecutive
months, individuals who at the beginning of such period constituted the board of
directors of the Borrower (together with any new directors whose election or
nomination was approved by a majority of the directors then in office) cease for
any reason to constitute a majority of the board of directors of the Borrower;
or (c) the Borrower ceases to own, directly or indirectly, 100% of the equity
interests of each of its subsidiaries.

"Commitment" means, collectively, the Revolving Commitment and the Term
Commitment.

"Guarantors" means each subsidiary of the Borrower that executes a Guaranty,
including Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC.

"Indebtedness" means, as to any Person, all obligations for borrowed money,
capital lease obligations, and other obligations of such Person evidenced by
notes, bonds or similar instruments.

"Loan Documents" means this Agreement, the Notes, the Guaranty, the Security
Documents and each other document executed in connection herewith.

"Obligations" means all advances, debts, liabilities, obligations and covenants
of the Borrower and the other Loan Parties owing to the Lender under the Loan
Documents, whether now existing or hereafter arising.

"Revolving Commitment" means the commitment of the Lender to make Revolving
Loans in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $15,000,000.

"Term Commitment" means the commitment of the Lender to make the Term Loan on
the Closing Date in an aggregate principal amount equal to $30,000,000.

"Term Loan Maturity Date" means January 17, 2030.

"Revolving Maturity Date" means January 17, 2028.

ARTICLE II - THE COMMITMENTS AND CREDIT EXTENSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2.01  The Term Loan.

    (a)  Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, the Lender made a
         single term loan to the Borrower on the Closing Date in the original
         principal amount of $30,000,000 (the "Term Loan"). The Term Loan was
         funded in a single drawing on January 17, 2023. Amounts repaid or
         prepaid in respect of the Term Loan may not be reborrowed.

    (b)  The Term Loan amortizes in equal quarterly installments of $375,000,
         payable on the last Business Day of each fiscal quarter, commencing
         March 31, 2023. The remaining unpaid principal balance of the Term Loan
         is due and payable on the Term Loan Maturity Date.

    (c)  As of December 31, 2024, the outstanding principal balance of the Term
         Loan was $26,300,000.

SECTION 2.02  The Revolving Loans.

    (a)  Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, the Lender agrees
         to make revolving loans (the "Revolving Loans") to the Borrower from
         time to time, up to but not exceeding the Revolving Commitment. Amounts
         borrowed and repaid under the Revolving Loans may be reborrowed.

    (b)  As of December 31, 2024, the outstanding principal balance of the
         Revolving Loans was $12,400,000.

SECTION 2.03  Interest.

    (a)  The Term Loan and the Revolving Loans bear interest at a rate per annum
         equal to the applicable SOFR rate plus the Applicable Margin, or at
         the Borrower's election, the Base Rate plus the Applicable Margin.

    (b)  Interest on the Loans is payable quarterly in arrears on the last
         Business Day of each fiscal quarter and on each maturity date.

SECTION 2.04  Optional Prepayments.

    (a)  The Borrower may, upon not less than five (5) Business Days' prior
         written notice to the Lender, prepay the Revolving Loans in whole or in
         part at any time without premium or penalty (other than customary
         breakage costs).

    (b)  The Borrower may, upon not less than five (5) Business Days' prior
         written notice to the Lender, prepay the Term Loan in whole or in part,
         in each case subject to Section 2.05.

SECTION 2.05  Prepayment Premium.

    (a)  If the Borrower prepays or repays any portion of the Term Loan
         (whether voluntarily, involuntarily, as a result of acceleration, or
         otherwise, and including in connection with any repayment required
         under Section 2.06), the Borrower shall pay to the Lender, concurrently
         therewith, a prepayment premium equal to 1.00% of the principal amount
         of the Term Loan then being prepaid or repaid, if such prepayment or
         repayment occurs on or prior to the third (3rd) anniversary of the
         Closing Date.

    (b)  No prepayment premium shall be payable in respect of any prepayment or
         repayment of the Term Loan occurring after the third (3rd) anniversary
         of the Closing Date.

    (c)  The third (3rd) anniversary of the Closing Date is January 17, 2026.

SECTION 2.06  Mandatory Prepayments; Change of Control.

    (a)  The Borrower shall repay the Loans in full, together with accrued and
         unpaid interest, all fees and any amounts payable under Section 2.05,
         on the date that is five (5) Business Days after the occurrence of a
         Change of Control.

    (b)  Upon and after the occurrence of a Change of Control, the Lender's
         commitments to make further Loans shall immediately terminate and the
         Lender shall have no obligation to make any further credit extensions.

    (c)  The Borrower shall give the Lender prompt written notice (and in any
         event within two (2) Business Days) of the occurrence of any Change of
         Control or of any event or circumstance that would reasonably be
         expected to result in a Change of Control.

ARTICLE VII - NEGATIVE COVENANTS (SELECTED PROVISIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 7.01  Indebtedness. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any
subsidiary to, create, incur or assume any Indebtedness, except for Permitted
Indebtedness.

SECTION 7.02  Liens. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit any subsidiary
to, create or permit to exist any Lien on any of its property, except for
Permitted Liens.

SECTION 7.03  Fundamental Changes. The Borrower shall not, and shall not permit
any subsidiary to, merge, consolidate, liquidate, dissolve or enter into any
transaction that results in a Change of Control, except as permitted under the
Loan Documents.

SECTION 7.04  Financial Covenants. The Borrower shall maintain, as of the last
day of each fiscal quarter:

    (a)  a maximum Total Leverage Ratio of 3.50 to 1.00; and

    (b)  a minimum Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio of 1.25 to 1.00.

ARTICLE VIII - EVENTS OF DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 8.01  Events of Default. Each of the following constitutes an Event of
Default:

    (a)  the Borrower fails to pay any principal of any Loan when due, or fails
         to pay any interest, fee or other amount within three (3) Business Days
         after the same becomes due;

    (b)  the Borrower fails to perform or observe any covenant contained in
         Article VII (other than as a result of a Change of Control permitted by
         the Lender);

    (c)  any representation or warranty made by any Loan Party proves to have
         been incorrect in any material respect when made;

    (d)  the occurrence of a Change of Control, subject to the Borrower's
         repayment obligations under Section 2.06; or

    (e)  the Borrower or any subsidiary becomes subject to any bankruptcy,
         insolvency or similar proceeding.

SECTION 8.02  Remedies. Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of an
Event of Default, the Lender may, among other things, declare all Obligations
immediately due and payable, terminate its commitments, and exercise all rights
and remedies available under the Loan Documents and applicable law.

SECTION 9.01  Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by, and construed in
