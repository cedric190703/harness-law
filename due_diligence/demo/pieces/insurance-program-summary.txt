7. ENVIRONMENTAL / POLLUTION LIABILITY (EIL / CONTRACTORS POLLUTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Insurer:            American Environmental Indemnity Company
    Policy No.:         AEI-EIL-2024-44012
    Policy Period:      July 1, 2024 - July 1, 2025
    Coverage Form:      Combined Environmental Impairment Liability and
                        Contractors Pollution Liability
    Limits:             $10,000,000 each loss; $10,000,000 policy aggregate
    Self-Insured        $100,000 each loss (claims-made basis)
    Retention:
    Coverage:           Third-party liability for bodily injury, property
                        damage and environmental remediation costs arising from
                        pollution conditions caused by the insured, including
                        sudden and accidental releases and certain gradual
                        conditions as expressly provided by the policy.
    Claims Basis:       Claims-made and reported.

    Key Exclusions and Limitations (summarized):

        (a)  Known Conditions. The policy does not provide coverage for any
             pollution condition, contamination, release or threatened release
             that was known to any insured prior to the policy inception date,
             or that is described in any notice, report or other document
             furnished to the insurer.

        (b)  Gradual Contamination at Owned Facilities. The policy excludes
             coverage for, and does not insure any liability, loss, cost or
             expense arising out of or in connection with, any gradual
             contamination, gradual pollution, gradual release or gradual
             seepage, migration or discharge of pollutants, contaminants or
             hazardous substances at or from any location or facility that is
             owned, leased or operated by any insured. For the avoidance of
             doubt, coverage is not provided for remediation of, or liability
             arising from, pre-existing or gradual contamination originating at
             owned locations, including contamination attributable to historical
             operations. Coverage under this policy is limited to pollution
             conditions arising from the insured's operations that are not
             otherwise excluded, as further set forth in the policy.

        (c)  Prior Acts / Retroactive Date. Coverage applies only to pollution
             conditions that commence on or after the retroactive date of
             July 1, 2019, subject to the terms of the policy.

        (d)  Other standard exclusions, including contractual liability
             assumed, fines and penalties (except defense costs where covered),
             and asbestos.
