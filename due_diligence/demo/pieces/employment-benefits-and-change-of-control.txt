This schedule summarizes certain employment, employee benefits, labor relations
and executive compensation matters relating to Rocky Environmental Services,
Inc. and its subsidiaries. Copies of the underlying plans, agreements and
notices are included in the data room under the "Employment" tab.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WORKFORCE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    -  Total employees: approximately 410
    -  Field technicians and operators: approximately 300
    -  Administrative and support: approximately 60
    -  Management and supervisory: approximately 50
    -  States of employment: Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, Idaho, Nevada, Montana

The Company's workforce is non-union as of the date hereof, subject to the
pending representation petition described in Section 5.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AND RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company has entered into employment agreements with each of its executive
officers. Each executive employment agreement contains customary terms regarding
base salary, bonus, benefits, confidentiality, non-competition, non-solicitation
and severance. Certain agreements provide for enhanced payments upon a
termination of employment in connection with a change of control, described in
Section 6.

5. LABOR RELATIONS - PENDING REPRESENTATION PETITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    5.1  On November 4, 2024, a representation (election) petition was filed
         with the National Labor Relations Board ("NLRB") seeking to represent a
         unit of the Company's field technicians.

    5.2  Petition details (as filed):
         -  Filing date:             November 4, 2024
         -  Affected employees:      62 field technicians
         -  Locations:               Grand Junction, Colorado and Casper, Wyoming
         -  Petitioner:              A labor organization
         -  Proposed unit:           Field technicians at the specified
                                     locations
         -  Status:                  Petition pending; the regional office is
                                     processing the petition, including any
                                     issues relating to the appropriate unit and
                                     eligibility.

    5.3  The Company is participating in the NLRB process in the ordinary course
         and is providing information requested by the regional office. The
         Company's response to the organizing effort is being handled in
         accordance with applicable law. The outcome of the petition process is
         not yet determined, and no collective bargaining agreement is currently
         in place with respect to the affected employees.

    5.4  The Company's workforce was non-union prior to the filing of the
         petition.
