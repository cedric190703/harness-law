1. HAZARDOUS WASTE PERMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1  Utah - Salt Lake City Transfer Station
     Permit No.:            UTH-0441
     Facility:              Salt Lake City Transfer Station
                            2100 West North Temple, Salt Lake City, UT 84116
     Permit Holder:         Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC
     Issuing Agency:        Utah Department of Environmental Quality (UDEQ),
                            Division of Waste Management and Radiation Control
     Regulatory Basis:      Utah Administrative Code R315-3 (Hazardous Waste
                            Management), including R315-3-3 through R315-3-5
                            (permit requirements and permit modifications), and
                            applicable provisions of 40 C.F.R. Part 270 as
                            incorporated by reference.
     Permit Type:           Hazardous waste transfer/storage facility permit
     Issued:                February 14, 2019
     Expiration:            February 13, 2029
     Status:                Active. Records reflect that the permit currently
                            remains in the name of Pinnacle Waste Solutions,
                            LLC. No final, formally issued permit transfer or
                            reissuance to Rocky Environmental Services, Inc. has
                            been located in the Company's files or in agency
                            records reviewed to date.

     Note: RES acquired Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC in March 2022. The Salt
     Lake City transfer station has been operated under the existing permit by
     personnel of the RES group since that time. The Company's environmental
     staff report that agency correspondence and periodic compliance submittals
     have been made under the existing permit number. The Company's files do not
     contain a final permit modification or transfer reflecting RES as the
     permit holder.

     Under R315-3 and applicable permitting practice, a change in the ownership
     or operational control of a permitted hazardous waste facility is generally
     addressed through permit modification procedures, and the manner in which
     such changes are documented can vary. The Company has not requested
     confirmatory written guidance from the agency regarding the current
     registration of this permit.

5. COMPLIANCE HISTORY AND NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1  General. The Company's EHS department maintains a compliance calendar,
     conducts periodic facility audits, provides employee training, and tracks
     regulatory inspections and correspondence. The Company believes its
     operations are conducted in substantial compliance with applicable
     environmental requirements.

5.2  Agency correspondence. From time to time, the Company receives inspection
     reports, information requests and notices from environmental agencies in
     the ordinary course. Certain regulatory matters are separately summarized in
     the litigation and regulatory matters schedule included in the data room.

5.3  Salt Lake City transfer station. Agency correspondence relating to the
     transfer station during the period since the Pinnacle acquisition has been
     addressed under permit number UTH-0441. The Company is reviewing the
     documentation of the permit holder of record in connection with the
     Transaction.

5.4  Wyoming Casper facility. The pending renewal of permit WY-HW-2019-033 is
     being handled in the ordinary course. The Company is not aware of any
     compliance issue affecting the facility's ability to continue operations
     during the renewal process.

5.5  Remediation accrual. The Company maintains an environmental remediation
     accrual of approximately $3,200,000 across all facilities combined, which
     management believes is adequate for its currently identified obligations.
