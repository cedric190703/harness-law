    Contract Ceiling:   $45,000,000
    Ceiling Remaining:  $12,300,000 (as of the date of this summary)
    TTM Revenue:        $24,400,000
    % of TTM Revenue:   19.2%
    Scope:              Environmental remediation, waste management and related
                        field services supporting government facilities and
                        remediation sites.

    1.1  General. The Contract incorporates the Federal Acquisition Regulation
         (FAR) by reference, including applicable clauses relating to the
         Government's rights and the Contractor's obligations.

    1.2  Assignment. The Contract is subject to the Anti-Assignment Act and
         FAR Subpart 42.12 (Novation and Change-of-Name Agreements). Under
         FAR 42.12, when a Contractor's assets are transferred, the transfer
         may give rise to the need for a novation agreement, and the transferee
         generally is not entitled to recognition as the successor to the
         Government contract unless and until a novation agreement is executed
         with the cognizant contracting officer. FAR 42.12 sets forth the
         circumstances in which a novation may be requested and the procedures
         for processing such requests.

    1.3  Successor contractors and government approvals. Consistent with FAR
         42.12 and applicable clauses, recognition of a successor contractor is
         subject to the discretion and approval of the contracting officer, and
         the Government may recognize a successor in interest only through a
         properly executed novation or change-of-name agreement. The Contract
         includes customary provisions regarding the Government's rights,
         termination for convenience, and the contracting officer's authority.

    1.4  Contracting officer rights. The contracting officer retains authority
         over contract administration, including with respect to any request for
         recognition of a successor contractor. The Company makes no
         representation regarding whether, or on what terms, a novation would be
         granted with respect to any transaction.

2. CONSOLIDATED MINING CORP. MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Customer:           Consolidated Mining Corp.
    Agreement:          Master Service Agreement
    Contract No.:       CMS-MSA-2019-114
    Effective Date:     March 1, 2019
    Term:               Initial term through February 28, 2025, with automatic
                        one-year renewals unless terminated
    TTM Revenue:        $14,500,000
    % of TTM Revenue:   11.4%
    Scope:              Waste management, industrial cleaning, remediation and
                        related field services at the customer's mining sites.

    2.1  General. The Master Service Agreement sets forth the framework terms
         under which the Company provides services pursuant to work orders,
         task authorizations and purchase orders issued from time to time. The
         agreement contains customary commercial terms, including scheduling,
         invoicing and payment, insurance and indemnity, change-order
         procedures, and health, safety and environmental requirements.

    2.2  Termination for convenience. The customer may terminate the agreement,
         or any work order issued thereunder, for convenience upon sixty (60)
         days' prior written notice to the Company. This termination-for-
         convenience provision is standard for agreements of this type.

    2.3  Termination for cause. Either party may terminate for material breach
         not cured within a specified cure period, subject to the terms of the
         agreement.

    2.4  Assignment. The agreement contains customary restrictions on assignment
         by the Company without the customer's prior written consent, subject to
         exceptions for assignments to affiliates and successors by operation of
         law.

    2.5  Renewal. The agreement renews automatically for successive one-year
         terms unless either party provides notice of non-renewal in accordance
         with the agreement.
