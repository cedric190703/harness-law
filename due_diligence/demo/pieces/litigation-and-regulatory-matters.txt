This schedule summarizes litigation, arbitration, governmental investigations and
regulatory matters involving Rocky Environmental Services, Inc. and its
subsidiaries. The descriptions below are based on information provided by the
Company and its outside counsel as of the date hereof. The Company's assessment
of each matter is preliminary and subject to change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CDPHE ENFORCEMENT MATTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1.1  Matter:            Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment
                            (CDPHE) - Hazardous Waste Enforcement

    1.2  Docket No.:        CDPHE-HW-2024-0093

    1.3  Agency:            Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment,
                            Hazardous Materials and Waste Management Division

    1.4  Facility:          Grand Junction Industrial Yard, Grand Junction,
                            Colorado

    1.5  Status:            Notice of enforcement received; discussions with the
                            agency are ongoing. No final compliance order or
                            penalty assessment has been issued.

    1.6  Nature:            The matter relates to alleged hazardous waste
                            management and recordkeeping deficiencies identified
                            by the agency in connection with the handling,
                            labeling and documentation of regulated materials at
                            the Grand Junction facility.

    1.7  Potential penalty. The applicable statutory maximum civil penalty is
         $37,500 per day of violation. The parties differ in their
         characterization of the number of days of alleged violation:

           -  The Company's position is that there were 12 violation days,
              implying an exposure of approximately $450,000 (12 x $37,500).

           -  The CDPHE's position, as most recently communicated, is that there
              were 31 violation days, implying an exposure of approximately
              $1,162,500 (31 x $37,500).

    1.8  Reserve. Management has not recorded a specific balance-sheet reserve
         for the penalties asserted by CDPHE in this matter.

    1.9  No EBITDA adjustment or balance-sheet reserve specifically relating to
         this matter has been reflected in the Company's financial statements or

2. RAMIREZ PERSONAL INJURY LAWSUIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    2.1  Case:              Ramirez v. Rocky Environmental Services, Inc.

    2.2  Case No.:          2:24-cv-00187

    2.3  Court:             U.S. District Court for the District of Wyoming

    2.4  Filed:             August 2024

    2.5  Plaintiff:         An individual alleging personal injury

    2.6  Claimed damages:   $4,800,000

    2.7  Nature of claim:   The plaintiff alleges personal injury arising from
         alleged exposure to contamination in connection with the Company's
         operations. The complaint asserts claims sounding in negligence and
         related theories.

    2.8  Company position:  The Company believes it has meritorious defenses,
         including challenges to the plaintiff's allegations of exposure,
         causation and the nature and extent of alleged injuries. The Company
         also asserts that its operations were conducted in accordance with
         applicable requirements and that coverage may be available under
         applicable policies. The Company intends to defend the matter
         vigorously.

    2.9  Status:            Discovery is ongoing. The matter remains unresolved,
         and no accrual has been recorded other than as may be reflected in the
         Company's general litigation reserve. The Company's view of this matter
         may change as the case develops.

3. ASBESTOS AND HISTORICAL OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    3.1  RES conducted asbestos abatement activities from 2012 through 2023.
         That service line was discontinued in 2023.

    3.2  The Company is not currently aware of any material active asbestos

         activities, and the potential for exposure claims associated with those

         future. The Company maintains records relating to its historical
         asbestos abatement work.

    3.3  No specific reserve has been recorded with respect to potential future
         asbestos-related claims.
