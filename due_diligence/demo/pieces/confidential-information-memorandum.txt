1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rocky Environmental Services, Inc. is a leading provider of environmental
services, hazardous and non-hazardous waste handling, remediation, industrial
field services and specialized transportation to a diversified base of
commercial, industrial and governmental customers across the Intermountain
West. Headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, the Company operates a network of
permitted facilities, transfer stations and field-service locations serving
customers in Utah, Colorado, Wyoming, Idaho, Nevada and Montana.

For the trailing twelve months ("TTM") ended September 30, 2024, the Company
generated revenue of approximately $127.1 million and Adjusted EBITDA of
$22.8 million. The Company's results reflect a resilient, recurring revenue
base, a highly experienced field workforce, a modern fleet and a demonstrated
ability to win and retain large, complex and technically demanding
environmental projects.

The Company is being offered for sale in a sale process being conducted by its
Financial Advisor, Certain Point Partners LLC. Management believes the Company
represents a compelling platform investment opportunity for a purchaser seeking
exposure to an essential, regulation-driven industry with significant
opportunities for organic growth and add-on acquisitions.

2. COMPANY OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Founded in 1994, RES has grown from a regional asbestos and lead abatement
contractor into a full-service environmental and industrial services platform.
The Company today provides a comprehensive suite of services spanning hazardous
and non-hazardous waste management, emergency response, industrial cleaning and
maintenance, soil and groundwater remediation, and specialized bulk and
roll-off transportation.

The Company serves a broad range of end markets, including federal and state

4. SERVICE LINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company delivers its services through six integrated service lines:

    (a)  Hazardous and Non-Hazardous Waste Management. Collection, packaging,
         transportation, treatment, stabilization and disposal of hazardous and
         non-hazardous industrial waste, including profiling, manifesting and
         brokered disposal. Approximately 34% of TTM revenue.

    (b)  Remediation and Environmental Construction. Soil and groundwater
         remediation, excavation, in-situ treatment, vapor intrusion mitigation
         and site restoration for industrial, energy and government clients.
         Approximately 22% of TTM revenue.

    (c)  Industrial and Field Services. Industrial cleaning, vacuum truck
         services, hydro-excavation, tank cleaning, confined-space entry and
         turnaround support. Approximately 21% of TTM revenue.

    (d)  Transportation and Logistics. Specialized bulk, roll-off, and
         over-the-road transportation of regulated and non-regulated materials.
         Approximately 13% of TTM revenue.

    (e)  Emergency Response. 24/7 emergency spill response, incident
         remediation and disaster recovery services. Approximately 6% of TTM
         revenue.

    (f)  Disposal and Treatment Facility Services. Operation of permitted
         transfer, treatment and storage facilities serving internal and
         third-party volumes. Approximately 4% of TTM revenue.

Note: The Company historically provided asbestos and lead abatement services
from 2012 through 2023. That service line has been fully exited, and the
Company no longer performs asbestos abatement or related regulated abatement
work.

6. ADJUSTED EBITDA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Management believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides a meaningful measure of the
Company's ongoing operating performance. For the trailing twelve months ended
September 30, 2024, Adjusted EBITDA was $22.8 million, as compared to reported
EBITDA of $18.4 million. Management's adjustments principally reflect items
that management considers to be non-recurring or non-operational in nature,
including owner compensation normalization, costs associated with the
Company's enterprise resource planning implementation, transaction-related
expenses arising from the Pinnacle acquisition integration, the wind-down of
the asbestos abatement service line, and a historical litigation settlement
unrelated to ongoing operations.

9. EMPLOYEES AND ORGANIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company employs approximately 410 people, including approximately 300 field

13. TECHNOLOGY AND SYSTEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company uses a suite of operational and back-office systems to manage its
business, including a recently implemented enterprise resource planning (ERP)
platform and a number of industry-specific tools. The Company is in the process
of enhancing its data and reporting capabilities to support future growth.

The Company has developed internally, through its own employees, a proprietary
operational software platform known as "RidgeTrack." RidgeTrack is used across
the Company's operations for dispatch, waste manifesting, route scheduling,
customer service and compliance record-keeping. RidgeTrack is owned and used by
the Company and is tailored to the Company's workflows, service lines and
regulatory requirements.

14. ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Company's operations are subject to extensive federal, state and local
environmental laws and regulations, including those governing the handling,
transportation, treatment, storage and disposal of hazardous and non-hazardous
waste, air emissions, water discharges and remediation of contaminated sites.

The Company holds a comprehensive portfolio of environmental permits and
approvals required to conduct its operations, including hazardous waste
permitting for its transfer and treatment facilities, air permits, stormwater
permits and transportation authorizations across the states in which it
operates. The Company is committed to maintaining compliance with all
applicable environmental requirements and invests in ongoing compliance,
training, monitoring and remediation.

The Company maintains a dedicated environmental, health and safety (EHS) team
and comprehensive environmental management systems. From time to time, the
Company receives inquiries and notices from regulatory agencies in the ordinary
course of its business, which management addresses on an ongoing basis.
