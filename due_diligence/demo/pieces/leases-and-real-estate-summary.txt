4. SALT LAKE CITY TRANSFER STATION - ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1  The lease for the Salt Lake City transfer station located at 2100 West
     North Temple was originally executed on June 1, 2018 by Wasatch Industrial
     Properties, LLC, as Landlord, and Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC, as Tenant.
     RES acquired Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC in March 2022. The transfer
     station has been occupied and used for transfer operations by personnel of
     the RES group since the acquisition.

4.2  A complete historical lease file for the Salt Lake City transfer station
     has not been located. The Company's files contain the original 2018 lease
     and amendments through 2021, together with rent invoices addressed to
     "Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC." No assignment, novation or other
     instrument transferring the tenant's interest in the lease to Rocky
     Environmental Services, Inc., and no written consent of the Landlord to
     such a transfer, has been located.

4.3  The lease contains the following provision (summarized):

        "Assignment and Subletting; Change of Control. Tenant shall not
        assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises, and
        shall not permit any transfer of a controlling interest in Tenant
        (whether by sale of equity, merger, consolidation or otherwise),
        in each case without the prior written consent of Landlord. Any
        assignment, sublease or transfer of control effected without such
        consent shall, at Landlord's option, constitute a default under
        this Lease. For purposes of this Section, a transfer of a
        controlling interest in Tenant includes any direct or indirect
        change in the ownership or control of Tenant, including by
        operation of a sale of all or substantially all of Tenant's
        equity or assets or of any parent entity."

4.4  Management has not obtained the Landlord's consent to any assignment of the
     lease or to the acquisition of Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC. Counsel is
     reviewing whether consent is required in connection with the Transaction.
     The Landlord has continued to accept rent and has not raised the
     assignment issue with the Company to date.
