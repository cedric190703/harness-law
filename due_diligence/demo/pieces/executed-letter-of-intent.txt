                            PROJECT RIDGELINE

                        December 19, 2024

Rocky Environmental Services, Inc.
3480 South Commerce Way, Suite 200
Salt Lake City, Utah 84104

Attention: Douglas R. Kessler, Chief Executive Officer

Re:  Non-Binding Letter of Intent to Acquire Certain Equity Interests of
     Rocky Environmental Services, Inc.

Dear Mr. Kessler:

This letter of intent (this "Letter") sets forth the principal terms and
conditions under which Sycamore Capital Partners ("Buyer" or "Sycamore"), or an
affiliate thereof, would acquire (the "Transaction") all of the outstanding
equity interests of Rocky Environmental Services, Inc., a Utah corporation (the
"Company" or "RES"), from the holders of such equity interests (each, a
"Seller" and collectively, the "Sellers"). This Letter is intended solely as an
expression of the parties' present intentions and, except as expressly provided
in Section 14 hereof, is not intended to be a legally binding obligation of any
party.

1.  TRANSACTION STRUCTURE

    1.1  Buyer proposes to acquire 100% of the issued and outstanding equity
         interests of the Company on a cash-free, debt-free basis, with the
         purchase price to be delivered at closing in cash, subject to the
         adjustments, holdback and other terms set forth herein.

    1.2  The Transaction is expected to be effected by way of a merger or equity
         purchase, the final structure to be determined by Buyer in
         consultation with its legal and tax advisors following completion of
         confirmatory diligence.

2.  PURCHASE PRICE

    2.1  Based on the Company's trailing twelve-month ("TTM") revenue of
         approximately $127.1 million and management's reported financial
         results, Buyer is prepared to pay an indicative enterprise value of
         between approximately $182 million and $205 million, subject to
         confirmation of quality of earnings, working capital, indebtedness and
         other confirmatory diligence.

    2.2  The enterprise value assumes a cash-free, debt-free structure. At
         closing, the Company will have no cash (other than a mutually agreed
         minimum operating amount) and no indebtedness, and the purchase price
         will be subject to adjustment for the actual amount of cash, the
         amount of indebtedness, and the amount by which closing working
         capital differs from an agreed target working capital amount.

    3.  HOLDBACK

    3.1  At closing, Buyer will withhold ten percent (10%) of the purchase price
         (the "Holdback Amount") as security for the Sellers' post-closing
         indemnification obligations under the definitive purchase agreement.

    3.2  Based on the indicative enterprise value range set forth above, the
         expected Holdback Amount is approximately $18.2 million to $20.5
         million.

4.  WORKING CAPITAL

    4.1  The Transaction will include a customary working capital mechanism. A
         target working capital amount will be agreed by the parties prior to
         execution of the definitive purchase agreement based on a normalized
         historical average of the Company's accounts receivable, inventory,
         prepaid expenses, accounts payable and accrued liabilities, in each
         case determined in accordance with the Company's historical accounting
         practices.

    4.2  The purchase price will be increased or decreased, as applicable, on a
         dollar-for-dollar basis to the extent that closing working capital is
         greater or less than the agreed target amount.

    4.3  "Net Debt" means, as of the closing, all outstanding indebtedness for
         borrowed money of the Company and its subsidiaries, together with
         accrued and unpaid interest thereon, capital lease obligations,
         letters of credit drawn and unreimbursed, and any other obligations of
         the type required to be reflected as indebtedness on a balance sheet
         prepared in accordance with GAAP.

5.  EXCLUSIVITY

    5.1  From the date of full execution of this Letter until the earlier of
         (a) execution of the definitive purchase agreement and (b) the date
         that is sixty (60) days following the date hereof (the "Exclusivity
         Period"), the Sellers and the Company will deal exclusively with Buyer
         with respect to the Transaction.

    5.2  During the Exclusivity Period, neither the Sellers nor the Company will,
         directly or indirectly, solicit, initiate, encourage or entertain any
         inquiry, proposal or offer from any person other than Buyer relating
         to any acquisition of all or a material portion of the equity or assets
         of the Company.

    5.3  The Exclusivity Period may be extended by mutual written agreement of
         the parties.

6.  DILIGENCE

    6.1  Following execution of this Letter, the Company will afford Buyer and
         its representatives, including its legal counsel Thornfield &
         Associates LLP and its accounting and environmental advisors,
         reasonable access to the Company's books, records, contracts,
         properties, facilities, personnel and other information for the purpose
         of conducting confirmatory legal, financial, environmental, tax,
         insurance, labor and regulatory diligence.

    6.2  Buyer's obligation to proceed is expressly conditioned upon the
         satisfactory completion of such diligence. The Company will make
         available a virtual data room containing the materials reasonably
         requested by Buyer.

7.  FINANCING

    7.1  Buyer intends to fund the Transaction through a combination of equity
         capital from Sycamore Capital Partners and affiliated investment
         vehicles and debt financing to be arranged with one or more banks or
         other financial institutions.

    7.2  Buyer's obligations under the definitive purchase agreement will not be
         conditioned upon obtaining financing.

8.  EMPLOYEE MATTERS

    8.1  Buyer expects to offer employment to substantially all of the Company's
         employees on terms and conditions, including base compensation and
         benefits, that are substantially comparable in the aggregate to those
         provided by the Company immediately prior to closing.

    8.2  The parties will cooperate in good faith to address employee benefit
         plan integration, continuation of service credit for vesting and
         eligibility purposes, and any required notices or filings.

    8.3  The Company will continue to operate its employee benefit plans in the
         ordinary course, consistent with past practice and applicable law,
         through the closing.

9.  DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT PROCESS

    9.1  The parties will negotiate in good faith with the goal of executing a
         definitive purchase agreement (including related transaction documents,
         disclosure schedules, an escrow agreement and such other agreements as
         are customary for a transaction of this size and type) as promptly as
         practicable following completion of confirmatory diligence.

    9.2  The definitive purchase agreement will contain customary representations
         and warranties, covenants, closing conditions and termination
         provisions.

    9.3  The representations and warranties will be subject to customary
         survival periods. General representations and warranties will survive
         the closing for eighteen (18) months. Environmental representations
         and warranties, and tax representations and warranties, will survive
         the closing for six (6) years.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

    10.1 The parties will continue to be bound by the terms of the mutual
         confidentiality agreement previously executed in connection with the
         evaluation of the Transaction, which remains in full force and effect.

    10.2 Neither party will disclose the existence or terms of this Letter or the
         Transaction to any third party, except to its advisors, financing
         sources, and as required by law, without the prior written consent of
         the other party.

11. GOVERNING LAW

    11.1 This Letter and the definitive purchase agreement will be governed by,
         and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Utah, without
         regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12. TRANSACTION EXPENSES

    12.1 Except as otherwise expressly provided, each party will bear its own
         fees, costs and expenses incurred in connection with the Transaction,
         including fees, costs and expenses of legal counsel, accountants,
         brokers, advisors, consultants and other representatives incurred in
         connection with the negotiation, documentation, financing and
         consummation of the Transaction (collectively, "Transaction
         Expenses").

    12.2 Certain Transaction Expenses may be treated as a reduction to the
         purchase price or as a Cash-Free/Debt-Free adjustment, as the parties
         may agree in the definitive purchase agreement.

13. INDEMNIFICATION

    13.1 The definitive purchase agreement will provide for customary
         indemnification by the Sellers of Buyer and its affiliates for breaches
         of representations, warranties and covenants, with the Holdback Amount
         serving as the first source of recovery.

    13.2 Indemnification will be subject to customary limitations, including
         deductibles, caps and exclusions, to be mutually agreed.

14. BINDING PROVISIONS

    14.1 Except for the provisions of Sections 5 (Exclusivity), 10
         (Confidentiality), 11 (Governing Law), 12 (Transaction Expenses) and
         this Section 14, which are intended to be binding on the parties, this
         Letter constitutes a statement of intent only and does not create any
         legally binding obligations on either party.

    14.2 The parties acknowledge that any obligation to consummate the
         Transaction will arise only upon execution and delivery of a definitive
         purchase agreement.
